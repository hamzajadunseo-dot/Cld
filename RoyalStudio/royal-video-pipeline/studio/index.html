Signed in as …

1 · Script

Paste the script, or choose a .txt / .docx file. The hook is everything before the first CHAPTER line (or before the first blank line if there are no chapters). Chapter titles are not read aloud.

2 · Voiceover

The finished narration (mp3 / wav). It must read the same script.

3 · Whose video is it?

Pick 1 person (the hook shows only them) or 2 for a couple video (e.g. Harry + Meghan). Guests named in the hook (Tyler Perry, Oprah…) still appear on their line.

4 · Make it

Takes about as long as the voiceover (25 min VO ≈ 25 min). If another video is running, yours waits in the queue. The locked style is applied automatically: hook = video clips only, navy lower-thirds & cards, no subtitles, clips first, no shot more than twice, fresh footage vs earlier videos. Audio = voiceover only (no music, no sound effects).

Replace the hook

Recorded a new hook? It replaces the old one: new hook voice + the old voiceover from chapter 1 onward, then the whole video is rendered again. The old voiceover, script and video are kept as _v1_oldhook.

Add the intro clip (locked style)

A real speech / news clip played before the narration: 3-framing montage, dark cinematic filter, gold word-synced quote text and a small archive tag. Saved as <name>_final.mp4.

Compilations (mp4) or photos of one person go into People\<Person>. Afterwards the library is indexed so face recognition knows who is in every shot (a 10-min compilation ≈ 3 min). New person? Type the name like Tyler_Perry.
